# Day 19 - Social Engineering

## What is Social Engineering?

Manipulating people into revealing information or performing actions.

## Targets

- Trust
- Fear
- Urgency
- Curiosity

## Types

### Phishing
Fake emails and websites.

### Pretexting
Fake story to gain trust.

### Baiting
Offering something attractive.

### Tailgating
Following someone into a restricted area.

## Protection

- Verify identities
- Use MFA
- Never share passwords or OTPs
- Report suspicious messages
