# Day 18 - Phishing

## What is Phishing?

A cyber attack that tricks users into revealing sensitive information.

## Targets

- Passwords
- OTPs
- Bank Details

## Common Techniques

- Fear
- Urgency
- Fake Rewards

## Warning Signs

- Strange sender address
- Suspicious links
- Requests for passwords
- Urgent messages

## Protection

- Verify links
- Verify sender
- Use MFA
- Avoid sharing passwords
