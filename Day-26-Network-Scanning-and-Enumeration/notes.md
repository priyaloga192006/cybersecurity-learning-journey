# Day 26 - Network Scanning and Enumeration

## Scanning

Finding devices, ports, and systems on a network.

## Enumeration

Gathering detailed information about discovered systems.

## Difference

Scanning = Discovery

Enumeration = Detailed Information Gathering

## Examples

Scanning:
- Find devices
- Find ports

Enumeration:
- Find services
- Find operating systems
- Gather detailed information

## Importance

Helps defenders understand and manage networks.
