# Day 9 - LAN vs WAN

## LAN

Full Form:
Local Area Network

A network within a small area such as a home, office, or school.

Examples:
- Home Wi-Fi
- Office Network

## WAN

Full Form:
Wide Area Network

A network that connects multiple LANs across large distances.

Example:
Internet

## Difference

LAN:
Local and small area.

WAN:
Large area and connects many networks.

## Command Practiced

ipconfig




**How to See the Difference**

**LAN Information**
ipconfig

**Shows:**

Laptop
↓
Router

(Local network)

**WAN Information**

**You can use:**

tracert google.com

**This shows traffic leaving your LAN:**

Laptop
↓
Router
↓
ISP
↓
Internet
↓
Google

**This gives you a glimpse of the WAN path.**
## Observation

The IPv4 address and Default Gateway belong to my local network (LAN).
