# Day 30 - Incident Response

## What is Incident Response?

The process of handling cybersecurity incidents.

## 6 Phases

1. Preparation
2. Identification
3. Containment
4. Eradication
5. Recovery
6. Lessons Learned

## Goals

- Minimize damage
- Restore operations
- Improve future security

## Example

Phishing
↓
Malware
↓
Detection
↓
Containment
↓
Recovery
