# Day 14 - CIA Triad

## Confidentiality

Only authorized users can access information.

Examples:
- Passwords
- OTPs
- Bank Details

## Integrity

Data should not be altered without authorization.

Example:
Changing exam marks illegally.

## Availability

Systems and data should be available when needed.

Examples:
- Websites
- Banking Systems
- Email Services

## CIA Triad

Confidentiality
Integrity
Availability
